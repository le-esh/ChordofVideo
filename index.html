How it works
  • 40×40 grid; a cell's brightness crossing one of 6 levels plays a note. Column = scale degree, row = octave (higher on screen = higher pitch).
  • Notes add energy to the bar above; full bar (or enough quiet time) advances to the next chord.
  • Progression 3 is interactive: top-of-frame motion → major, bottom → minor.
  • Propagation (optional): a fired cell can nudge neighbors into firing too, quietly, rippling outward for a few seconds. Wrap edges makes the grid a torus, so ripples can cross from one edge to the opposite one.
  • Irrational engine (optional): a digit stream (π, e, √2 or φ) pushes propagation stronger or weaker as it advances — and the screen's own activity sets how fast that stream moves, so the grid and the digits keep nudging each other.
